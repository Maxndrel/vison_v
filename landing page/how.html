How It Works

Swapping your electronics on Vision V is simple, fast, and secure. Here’s how:

1. Upload Your Device

Take clear photos of your device from multiple angles. Include details like brand, model, specifications, condition, and any accessories. Our AI-powered system helps categorize your listing automatically.

Pro tip: High-quality photos get 3x more views!
2. Choose a Device to Swap

Use our advanced search filters to find the perfect match. Browse by category, location, condition, and price range. Make an offer directly through our secure messaging system.

Browse thousands of verified listings daily.
3. Confirm and Swap

Once you agree on terms, arrange delivery or pickup. Our escrow system holds funds securely until both parties confirm receipt. Rate your experience to build trust.

100% secure transactions guaranteed.
