How It Works

Swapping your electronics on Vision V is simple, fast, and secure. Here’s how:

1. Upload Your Device

Take a photo of your device and upload it with details like model, specs, and condition.

2. Choose a Device to Swap

Browse available listings and find the perfect device to exchange with yours.

3. Confirm and Swap

Agree to the swap, arrange delivery or pickup, and confirm the exchange.

Stay safe and secure while swapping devices on Vision V:
